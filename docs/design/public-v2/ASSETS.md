Supply at least 1200px-wide originals where appropriate for desktop; export responsive derivatives. Keep labels/arrows in HTML. Do not enlarge the small reference crops and call them production assets.

Practitioner: actual approved portrait with enough negative space for alternate mobile/desktop crops; separate genuine introduction-video poster if video exists. Programmes: approved authored educational artwork and accurate content previews, only when a real programme exists.
