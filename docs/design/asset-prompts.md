# Visual production brief and future asset prompts

These are briefs for later asset work, not claims that new production assets were generated in this handoff. The current final mockup remains a style reference. Prefer real approved photographs for Charlie and the clinic.

## Shared service-image direction

Create a coherent three-image editorial healthcare series for PhysiX. Quiet off-white environment, muted teal/sage wardrobe and restrained mint background shapes. Natural soft light, neutral skin rendering, realistic proportions, anatomically credible posture, consistent camera height and contrast. Crop safely for a 4:3 service-card image with generous room around the relevant movement. No typography, UI, watermarks, logos, claims, badges, decorative plants competing with the subject, or fake clinical before/after comparison. Assets must look like one intentionally art-directed set, not a collage of stock-photo, cartoon and shiny 3D styles.

### Sports rehabilitation

An adult performing a controlled, believable rehabilitation movement in a neutral exercise environment, tasteful teal athletic clothing and simple equipment only where relevant. Movement selected/checked by the clinician. Convey calm coached activity rather than extreme athletic performance or a promise of recovery. Avoid exaggerated joints, duplicate limbs and cropped-out relevant contact points.

### Manual therapy

A respectful close crop of a clinician's hands during an approved hands-on assessment/treatment setup, with patient positioning and anatomy reviewed by the clinician. Matching light and palette to the sports image. Do not imply a particular technique is suitable for every symptom. Hands, fingers, joints and skin contact need detailed visual inspection.

### Guided programme illustration

An adult following a simple clinician-approved home exercise setup with uncluttered space. Match the first image's photographic/editorial treatment and framing. A programme booklet can appear only as a neutral unlabelled prop; actual lesson counts, product name and benefits live in HTML after approval. Do not use the illustration as proof of individualized clinical review.

## Body-area set

Produce a consistent set of five simplified non-diagnostic silhouettes/body-region illustrations. Neutral pale material, clear but restrained teal highlight at back, neck, shoulder, knee and posture/spine respectively. Same lighting/framing/line weight and highlight size. No frightening red glow, visible injury, diagnosis labels or pseudo-scan artifacts. Text labels remain separate HTML. Clinician reviews anatomical correspondence before publishing.

## Practitioner photography brief

Photograph the real Charlie with permission, approachable and professional, even light, natural expression, neutral background, high-resolution original and flexible portrait crop. Capture a few orientations for narrow/mobile and wide layouts. Use truthful clothing/credentials; no generated qualification embroidery. Retain natural appearance; do not substitute a synthetic look-alike for identity photography.

## Quality review before accepting any image

Inspect anatomy, hands, limb count, contact points, exercise positioning, consistent visual style, crops at 320/390/1440 widths, contrast behind HTML, image dimensions and file size. Confirm source/permission and correct demo-versus-approved status. Reject near-miss assets rather than hiding mistakes beneath gradients. A nice generated card is not a finished asset library.

Do not generate the entire homepage as a single production image. Generate individual imagery only; implement copy, states, controls, hover/focus, responsive layout and accessibility in code.
