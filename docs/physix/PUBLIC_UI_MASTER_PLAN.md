# PhysiX public UI master plan

Status: current design/implementation contract for the public website after PX-062. This document exists to prevent another visual-direction reset.

## Product boundary

PhysiX is the public clinic and booking website. It introduces the clinic, helps visitors discover services, supports booking, explains online care, and provides a clear My account entry.

Gymaf is the retained signed-in member experience for courses, programmes, exercises, progress and account features. Do not redesign, recolor or replace it during public-site work.

The public website and Gymaf may share identity, data and navigation entry points, but they are not the same interface.

## Visual direction that survives

Keep the mint / white / navy / deep-teal PhysiX family. Purple and charcoal public-site concepts are rejected.

The current issue tiles and service-card direction are the strongest approved-by-feedback parts of the homepage. Preserve their visual language unless the owner explicitly asks to change them.

The current homepage is improved but still reviewable. Header, hero, Charlie/profile treatment and FAQ can continue to be polished under the same family; do not restart the whole page.

The supplied original mint reference and the best later Image Gen concepts are visual evidence, not permission to rasterize an interface or invent clinic facts.
## Booking IA correction — 9 September 2026
Services remain discoverable from Home and service-detail pages, but they are not a primary dock destination. Appointment-service selection starts inside Book. The public dock remains Home / Book / Online / Account, and booking uses a focused shell without that dock competing with the booking action.
